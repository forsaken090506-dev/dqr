Harvard Labs V4.2 Hardcore Review (experimental)

Changes from V4.1:
- Hardcore preset disables arena-clear resets and post-Bob resets, in addition to the existing resetInHardcore=false gate. This prevents those two intentional reset paths from being enabled by the preset.
- Bounded damage history (up to 80 recent sampled health drops) with health percentages, timestamps, and character root coordinates to help diagnose risky areas.
- Death timestamps and version marker in _G.HL_V4_HC_REPORT.

Enable before loading:
getgenv().HL_V4_HARDCORE = true

After testing, inspect:
_G.HL_V4_HC_REPORT
_G.HL_V4_HC_REPORT.damageEvents

Limitations:
- Damage is sampled every 0.25 seconds; multiple hits between samples can merge and fast deaths/respawns may be missed.
- The original movement and hazard algorithms are not rewritten.
- Not run or validated inside Roblox; no claim of deathless runs.
- Review third-party code before executing it.
